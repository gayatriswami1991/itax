What does a PCRT compliant R&D tax adviser do?

A PCRT compliant adviser follows the profession's conduct standards for tax work. On an R&D claim that means checking your projects against HMRC's definition of R&D, tying costs to qualifying activities, keeping the evidence for both, and telling you plainly where a position is weak.     What does PCRT compliant mean for an R&D tax adviser?

    PCRT is Professional Conduct in Relation to Taxation, the standard set by the UK's main accountancy and tax bodies for members who give tax advice. A PCRT compliant adviser works to those rules on honesty, competence and confidentiality, and does not promise outcomes they cannot control.

    How do I know if my project qualifies as R&D for tax?

    HMRC looks for a project that seeks an advance in science or technology by resolving scientific or technological uncertainty that a competent professional in the field could not readily work out. Being new or complex for your business is not enough on its own.

    Can you give an R&D tax claim second opinion on a claim another adviser prepared?

    Yes. We review filed and draft claims independently, covering eligibility, cost treatment and evidence, and we tell you where the position is strong and where it needs work. See the second opinion service.

    What should I do if HMRC opens an enquiry into my R&D claim?

    Do not ignore the letter, and note the response date it gives. Gather the project records and cost workings, and speak to an adviser before you reply. Our HMRC R&D enquiry support handles the written responses and the evidence.

    What are the contracted out R&D rules?

    For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024, HMRC's guidance says the general principle is to identify the party that decided to initiate the R&D, the one that intended or contemplated that R&D when entering the contract. That is not always the party that did the work. Financial risk, IP ownership and who will exploit the results are also considered. Read our guide to the contracted out R&D rules.

    Is there a difference between R&D tax advisers, advisors and consultants?

    No. R&D tax advisers, R&D tax advisors and R&D tax consultants are used interchangeably in the UK, and the spelling varies. What matters is who is behind the label: a qualified specialist who signs off the claim, works to PCRT standards, holds professional indemnity insurance and is supervised for anti-money laundering. Ask any R&D tax adviser UK firm you speak to for those four things.
