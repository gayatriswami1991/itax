What does a PCRT compliant R&D tax adviser do?

A PCRT compliant adviser follows the profession's conduct standards for tax work. On an R&D claim that means checking that your projects meet HMRC's definition of R&D, tying costs to qualifying activities, keeping the evidence for both, and telling you plainly where a position is weak.

Questions we are asked most

What does PCRT compliant mean for an R&D tax adviser?

PCRT is Professional Conduct in Relation to Taxation, the standard set by the UK's main accountancy and tax bodies for members who give tax advice. A PCRT compliant adviser works to those rules on honesty, competence and confidentiality, and does not promise outcomes they cannot control.

How do I know if my project qualifies as R&D for tax?

HMRC looks for a project that seeks an advance in science or technology by resolving scientific or technological uncertainty that a competent professional in the field could not readily work out. Being new or complex for your business is not enough on its own.

Can you review an R&D claim another adviser prepared?

Yes. We review filed and draft claims independently, covering eligibility, cost treatment and evidence, and we tell you where the position is strong and where it needs work.

What should I do if HMRC opens an enquiry into my R&D claim?

Do not ignore the letter, and note the response date it gives. Gather the project records and cost workings, and speak to an adviser before you reply. Our enquiry support service handles the written responses and the evidence.

Who can claim contracted-out R&D?

Under the merged scheme, which applies to accounting periods starting on or after 1 April 2024, the customer that commissions the R&D generally claims, not the contractor. Contract terms, overseas work and group arrangements can change this, so check before you claim.
