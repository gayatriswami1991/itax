Our story

Why we built a better way to prepare claims

With increasing reporting requirements, heightened HMRC scrutiny and extensive evidence requests even for the smallest claim details, preparing R&D claims has become increasingly time-consuming and costly for businesses.

We therefore set out to streamline the claims preparation process but recognised that automation alone is not sufficient. Businesses need expert advice to navigate the complexities around R&D eligibility and costs. Additionally, they must be able to demonstrate to HMRC and other stakeholders how they have been compliant.

Our claim approach combines expert support with a powerful platform, enabling businesses to leverage automation while maintaining access to professional guidance. As businesses grow more sophisticated, many seek to integrate R&D tax claims into their regular financial reporting. We support this transition, helping companies forecast and plan with confidence.

Meet our founder, Aarti Varia

Aarti is the founder of iTax Advisors and a specialist UK R&D tax adviser with over 18 years’ experience helping businesses navigate the R&D tax relief regime.

Her career includes 13 years at Deloitte, where she advised FTSE 100, FTSE 250 and privately owned businesses across sectors including technology, engineering, manufacturing, life sciences and construction. She subsequently held senior specialist R&D tax roles before establishing iTax Advisors.

Drawing on her technical tax expertise and external audit background, Aarti specialises in developing robust claim methodologies, interpreting complex financial and operational data, and preparing high-quality R&D tax claims supported by clear and defensible evidence.

Aarti works closely with senior management, finance and technical teams to identify qualifying activities, strengthen supporting documentation and implement practical processes that improve the capture of R&D information for both current and future claims.

The strongest R&D tax claims are no longer simply those that identify qualifying activities. They are often those that can clearly demonstrate how conclusions were reached and supported.
