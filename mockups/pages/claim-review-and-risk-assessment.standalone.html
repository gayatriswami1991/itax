What we review

What we check in an R&D tax claim review

Our R&D tax claim review focuses on the technical, financial and evidential strength of the claim, so the numbers and the technical story are reviewed together.

Project eligibility

Did the project seek an advance in overall knowledge or capability in a field of science or technology?

Common issue: routine work included, or business problems described as technical ones.

Technical narrative

Does it explain the starting point, the advance sought, the specific uncertainty and the work undertaken?

Common issue: the commercial advance is described instead of the technological one.

Qualifying costs

Do contracts, invoices and work performed support the treatment of costs, and how was staff time apportioned?

Common issue: apportionments are not clearly evidenced.

Supporting evidence

Do dated records such as test results, design notes and payroll records support what the claim says?

Common issue: gaps, or records written after the event.

Filing and compliance

Do the claim, the Additional Information Form and the Company Tax Return agree?

Common issue: narrative and cost schedule tell different stories.

R&D tax claim second opinion questions

Short answers first.

Do we need an independent review if we already have an adviser?

Not always. However, an independent review can be valuable where the claim is material, technically complex or where directors want a stronger understanding of compliance risk and evidential quality.

Can you review historic R&D tax claims?

Yes. We can review previously submitted claims to identify weaknesses, assess exposure, support remediation planning and help businesses decide whether corrective action may be appropriate.

Can this help reduce HMRC enquiry risk?

Yes. A stronger evidential position, clearer technical analysis and improved governance can significantly improve claim defensibility and preparedness.

Who provides independent project reviews to reduce the risk of R&D claims?

iTax Advisors provides independent R&D tax claim review and risk assessment. We review the technical narratives, qualifying costs and evidence behind existing or proposed claims, and give practical recommendations to improve compliance and claim defensibility.

Will a second opinion upset my current adviser?

It shouldn’t. Good advisers expect their work to be reviewed by auditors, investors and HMRC. An independent review simply adds another check, and many clients share the findings with their existing adviser.

How long does an R&D tax claim second opinion take?

For a typical SME claim with two to five projects, a review usually takes two to three weeks. Much depends on how quickly we can speak to the technical team.

Does a review stop HMRC opening an enquiry?

Nothing removes the risk entirely. A review does mean the claim is better evidenced, and that you know its weak points before HMRC asks about them.

What types of businesses use this service?

Our clients include SMEs and larger organisations seeking a more rigorous review of claim quality and compliance exposure.
