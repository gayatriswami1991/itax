Your HMRC R&D enquiry letter

Which HMRC R&D enquiry letter have you received?

Not every letter from HMRC about an R&D claim is a formal enquiry, and the right response depends on which one you have. Check the heading and the legislation the letter refers to before you do anything else. If you cannot tell, ask the officer named on the letter to confirm.

An enquiry is not a final decision that the claim is wrong. HMRC will consider the explanation and evidence before reaching an outcome.

How far back can HMRC look?

HMRC can usually open an enquiry within 12 months of the date the corporation tax return was filed, as long as it was filed on time. If the R&D claim was made or changed by amending the return, the window generally runs from the amendment instead. Once that window has closed, HMRC needs to raise a discovery assessment, which is normally limited to 4 years from the end of the accounting period, extended to 6 years where an inaccuracy was careless and 20 years where it was deliberate.

The first few days

HMRC R&D compliance check: what to do in the first 48 hours

An HMRC R&D enquiry can feel urgent, but a calm, organised approach will help you protect deadlines, preserve evidence and prepare a more credible response.

Start with a one-page control sheet
1

Create a control sheet

Record the deadline, accounting period, projects in scope, HMRC contact, internal owner and location of the supporting records.

2

Preserve the evidence

Secure relevant emails, project files, timesheets, payroll data, invoices and claim workings. Do not overwrite originals.

3

Nominate one coordinator

One person coordinates contact with HMRC so technical, finance and adviser responses do not diverge.

4

Reconcile the claim as filed

Obtain the final corporation tax return, computations, additional information form, technical narrative and cost schedule actually filed, not an earlier draft.

5

Check the scope of the request

Identify the questions, documents, accounting periods and projects covered. Seek advice if HMRC later issues a formal information notice.

6

Keep your other deadlines

Keep filing returns and paying other taxes by their normal deadlines. If an adviser deals with HMRC, check that authority is in place.

Life sciences

HMRC R&D enquiries for life sciences companies in London, Oxford and Cambridge

Life sciences companies in London, Oxford and Cambridge often work with contract research organisations, university labs and clinical trial sites. That makes their enquiries a little different from a typical software case, because HMRC tends to ask about the science and about who was really doing the R&D.

The answer has to come from the lead scientist, not the finance team. Work done by the CRO is pulled out and looked at separately, because it has to be tested against the contracted-out R&D rules for the relevant period. Payments to clinical trial volunteers are matched to the trial agreements.

What does HMRC usually ask about in an R&D enquiry?

HMRC commonly questions whether the work meets the definition of R&D for tax purposes, whether genuine technological uncertainties existed, whether the projects sought an advance in science or technology, and whether the qualifying expenditure can be properly supported. Enquiry letters can be detailed and request substantial technical and financial information relating to the submitted claim.

Can you help if another adviser prepared the claim?

Yes. We regularly assist companies where the original R&D claim was prepared by another adviser. We can independently review the submission, identify potential risk areas and provide structured support in preparing and managing the enquiry response.

Can HMRC reject an entire claim?

Yes. If HMRC believes the projects or expenditure do not meet the legislative requirements, part or all of a claim may be denied. A clear and technically supported response can materially improve the quality of the defence.

How far back can HMRC open an R&D enquiry?

HMRC can usually open an enquiry within 12 months of the date the corporation tax return was filed, as long as it was filed on time. Once that window has closed, HMRC needs to raise a discovery assessment, normally limited to 4 years from the end of the accounting period, extended to 6 years where an inaccuracy was careless and 20 years where it was deliberate.

Is an HMRC R&D enquiry a final decision that my claim is wrong?

No. An enquiry is not a final decision that the claim is wrong. HMRC will consider the explanation and evidence before reaching an outcome.

Do I have to reply to a “nudge” letter?

A nudge or one-to-many letter asks you to check your claim yourself. It is not an enquiry, so a reply is usually not required, but HMRC may open an enquiry later if it still has concerns. Review the claim honestly and correct any errors you find.
