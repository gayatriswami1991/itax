How to claim R&D tax relief: the three steps

Short answer: check whether you must notify HMRC, submit the Additional Information Form, then make the claim in your CT600.

The claim itself is submitted through the company’s Corporation Tax Return (CT600), but several steps must be completed before submission.

Step 1: Check whether you need to notify HMRC

Short answer: companies may need to submit a claim notification form before making an R&D claim, for accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2023.

You must notify HMRC if:

  • you are making an R&D claim for the first time; or
  • your last R&D claim was made more than three years before the end of the current claim notification period; or
  • you claimed for the previous accounting period but submitted it within an amended return received on or after 1 April 2023, or HMRC rejected the claim by removing it from the Company Tax Return.

What is the claim notification period? It begins on the first day of the accounting period and ends six months after the end of the accounting period. Failure to notify where required may result in your claim being rejected.

HMRC’s claim notification form (gov.uk)

Step 2: Submit the Additional Information Form (AIF)

Short answer: the AIF is mandatory for all R&D claims and must be submitted before the CT600 is filed.

HMRC will reject claims where the form has not been submitted before the CT600 filing. It requires:

  • information about the company;
  • a breakdown of qualifying expenditure;
  • details of the projects being claimed;
  • an explanation of the scientific or technological advances sought; and
  • contact details for the claim.

How many projects must you describe?

Projects claimedWhat you must describe
1–3 projectsAll projects, covering 100% of qualifying expenditure
4–10 projectsAt least 3 projects, and projects representing at least 50% of total qualifying expenditure
11 or moreProjects representing at least 50% of qualifying expenditure, and the 10 projects with the highest qualifying expenditure where appropriate

For each project, HMRC expects technical descriptions, details of the scientific or technological uncertainties and a cost breakdown, plus contact details for responsible individuals and advisers.

HMRC’s Additional Information Form guidance (gov.uk)

Step 3: Make the claim in the CT600

Short answer: once notification (where required) and the AIF are complete, the claim can be submitted within the Company Tax Return.

It is important that the claim is accurate, robust and supported by sufficient technical and financial evidence, to reduce the risk of HMRC enquiries. A properly prepared claim based on genuine qualifying activities should not be viewed as high risk, but HMRC scrutiny has increased significantly in recent years.

  • Technically robust and evidence-based
  • Properly documented
  • Prepared by experienced professionals

Aggressive or poorly supported claims can lead to enquiries, penalties or repayment demands. See claim review and risk assessment for an independent check.

R&D tax claim deadline and retrospective claims

Short answer: the claim deadline is generally two years from the end of the relevant accounting period.

  • Companies can generally amend a corporation tax return to include an R&D claim within two years from the end of the accounting period.
  • For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2023, many companies must also submit a claim notification form before making the claim.
  • Companies can claim every year, provided they continue to undertake qualifying R&D.

Frequently asked questions

How do I claim R&D tax relief?

Check whether you need to submit a claim notification, submit the mandatory Additional Information Form, then make the claim in the Corporation Tax Return (CT600).

When is the R&D tax claim deadline?

Generally two years from the end of the relevant accounting period. A claim notification, where required, must be made within six months of the end of the accounting period.

Do I need to notify HMRC before making a claim?

Possibly. For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2023, you must notify if it is your first claim, if your last claim was made more than three years before the end of the current notification period, or in the amended-return situations HMRC specifies.

Is the Additional Information Form mandatory?

Yes, for all R&D claims. HMRC will reject claims where the AIF was not submitted before the CT600 was filed.

How many projects must I describe in the AIF?

With 1–3 projects, all of them. With 4–10, at least 3 projects and at least 50% of qualifying expenditure. With 11 or more, projects representing at least 50% of qualifying expenditure, and the 10 highest where appropriate.

Can I make a retrospective R&D claim?

Yes. Companies can generally amend a corporation tax return to include an R&D claim within two years from the end of the relevant accounting period.

Can companies claim R&D tax relief every year?

Yes, provided they continue to undertake qualifying R&D activities. Many businesses claim repeatedly as innovation evolves.
