What is the merged R&D expenditure credit scheme (RDEC)?

For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024, most companies claim under the merged R&D expenditure credit scheme. It provides a taxable credit equal to 20% of qualifying R&D expenditure, giving a net benefit of 15% where the 25% main rate of corporation tax applies and 16.2% where the 19% small profits rate applies. Loss-making SMEs that meet the R&D intensity threshold and the other qualifying conditions may instead claim Enhanced R&D Intensive Support (ERIS).

What must we submit to make an R&D tax relief claim?

Every claim requires an Additional Information Form before, or on the same day as, the Company Tax Return is submitted. Some companies must also submit a claim notification form within the applicable time limit. Eligibility for the underlying R&D does not remove these procedural requirements, so the filing position should be checked early.
