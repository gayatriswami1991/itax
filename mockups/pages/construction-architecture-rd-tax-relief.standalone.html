In short

Can construction companies claim R&D tax relief?

Yes, where the work sought an advance in science or technology by resolving uncertainty a competent professional could not readily resolve. Complexity, novelty or bespoke design alone does not qualify, and you must also establish who is entitled to claim.

Site labour and subcontractors

  • Ordinary excavation, installation, assembly and other construction work do not qualify just because they sit on a project involving R&D
  • Costs must link to the R&D activities
  • Staff, externally provided workers and contractor payments each follow their own rules

Overseas subcontractors

  • Overseas subcontractor and externally provided worker costs are restricted under the merged scheme
  • Only narrow exceptions apply, so establish where the work happened and the engagement model

FAQs

Construction R&D tax relief questions

Short answers first. Speak to us about your own position.

Unsure who can claim?Ask a specialist
Can construction companies claim R&D tax relief?

Yes, where qualifying scientific or technological R&D is undertaken and the claim conditions are met. Complex projects, bespoke designs and difficult site conditions do not automatically qualify.

Can a subcontractor claim R&D tax relief?

Potentially. Under the merged scheme, entitlement depends on the contracted-out R&D rules, including whether the customer intended or contemplated the R&D. Where the customer contracted it out, the customer will generally be entitled to claim, subject to exceptions.

Can construction companies claim for site labour?

Only where the activities relate to eligible R&D and the spend satisfies the statutory rules. Ordinary excavation, installation and assembly do not qualify merely because they take place on a project involving R&D.

Does sustainable construction qualify?

Potentially, where developing new or enhanced technologies, materials or engineering methods seeks an advance. Simply installing established sustainable products or meeting environmental standards does not automatically qualify.

Can unusual ground conditions or flood risks lead to qualifying R&D?

Potentially, but difficult site conditions alone are not sufficient. There must be an advance sought in science or technology with uncertainty that could not readily be resolved using established engineering knowledge.

Does planning permission or regulatory approval establish R&D eligibility?

No. Planning approval, building regulations compliance and other regulatory decisions do not themselves establish scientific or technological advancement. Eligibility depends on the underlying technological work.
