In short

What is R&D tax relief for life sciences companies?

It is non-dilutive funding for qualifying R&D, at a point when cash directly affects a company’s ability to progress research, retain scientific capability and extend its development runway. Qualifying work arises across discovery, clinical development, diagnostics, medical technology and bioprocess scale-up, but not every activity qualifies.

Common challenges

CRO R&D tax relief and more: three challenges for life sciences companies

Short answer: overseas costs, who is entitled to claim under CRO and CDMO contracts, and evidencing the claim without burdening scientists. Selective funding, longer timelines and investor scrutiny make timing, value and defensibility more important, and more complex.

Will our overseas clinical trials, CRO and specialist laboratory costs still qualify?

The challenge
  • Research is often international: trial recruitment, specialist facilities, manufacturing capabilities, regulatory considerations
  • For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024, restrictions on overseas contractor and externally provided worker costs can substantially reduce claims
How iTax Advisors helps
  • Map where each activity took place, who performed it and how costs were incurred
  • Assess the statutory overseas exception, including whether replicating the conditions in the UK would have been wholly unreasonable
  • Separate genuinely restricted spend from costs that may still qualify. Qualifying costs guide

Who is entitled to claim when R&D is delivered through CROs, CDMOs or research partners?

The challenge
  • Under the merged scheme, entitlement can depend on whether the customer intended or contemplated that R&D when the contract was entered into
  • CRO and CDMO agreements may not record who initiated the R&D, controlled the programme, bore the risk or expected to claim
How iTax Advisors helps

  • Connect the scientific activities to the financial records. Contracted-out R&D rules

The challenge

Most companies, from 1 April 2024

  • For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024
  • Credit is applied against corporation tax first, then passes through statutory payment steps
  • Any remaining amount may be payable in cash, subject to restrictions including the PAYE and NIC cap
Enhanced R&D Intensive Support (ERIS)

Loss-making R&D-intensive SMEs

86%additional deduction on qualifying R&D expenditure
14.5%payable credit rate on the surrendered loss
26.97%maximum cash benefit, with sufficient losses
  • Meets the R&D intensity threshold and other conditions
  • Qualifying R&D generally at least 30% of total relevant expenditure (a one-year grace period may apply)

Payment timing is not fixed. It depends on:

  • When the accounts and return are submitted
  • Whether the claim notification and Additional Information Form are completed correctly. How to make a claim

FAQs

Life sciences R&D tax relief questions

Short answers first. Speak to us about your own position.

Unsure how your programme is treated?Ask a specialist
Can clinical trial expenditure qualify for R&D tax relief?

Yes. Activities undertaken during clinical development may qualify where they directly contribute to resolving scientific or technological uncertainty. Read the clinical trial costs guide.

Can we claim expenditure incurred through overseas CROs?

Potentially. Overseas contractor expenditure is subject to restrictions for accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024, although statutory exceptions may apply.

Does grant funding prevent us from claiming R&D tax relief?

Under the new merged scheme (applies to accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024), receiving a grant does not in itself disqualify expenditure from relief. However, contractual arrangements, who undertakes or initiates the R&D and other eligibility conditions must still be considered.

Can loss-making biotech companies receive R&D tax relief?

Yes. Loss-making R&D-intensive SMEs may qualify for Enhanced R&D Intensive Support (ERIS), subject to the applicable intensity threshold and other conditions.

Who can claim when a sponsor commissions work from a CRO or CDMO?

For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024, entitlement generally depends on which party intended or contemplated the relevant R&D when the contract was entered into. The contract is important, but HMRC may also consider the wider facts, including technical control, financial risk, IP arrangements and the parties’ understanding of the work. Read about the contracted-out R&D rules.

How much R&D tax relief could we receive, and when?

The amount and timing depend on the company’s accounting period, tax position, qualifying expenditure and the scheme under which it claims. For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024, most companies claim under the merged R&D expenditure credit scheme, a taxable credit equal to 20% of qualifying R&D expenditure.
