What software development qualifies for R&D tax relief?

Software qualifies when the work seeks an advance in the wider field of technology by addressing genuine technological uncertainties that a competent professional could not readily resolve. Work that only delivers new or improved commercial functionality does not.

What qualifies

Software R&D tax credits UK: what counts as qualifying R&D?

  • Software development needs substantial technical expertise, complex architecture and significant spend
  • But not all software development qualifies
  • The test is whether the work only delivers new or improved functionality, or seeks an advance in technology by resolving genuine uncertainty
  • Eligibility depends on the specific advances and uncertainties, not simply the use of sophisticated technology
Technological advanceUncertaintyCompetent professionalEvidence
Advance in technologyNot routineUncertainty

May qualify

  • New or significantly enhanced approaches to architecture, scalability, concurrency or resilience
  • AI and ML work that advances algorithms, methods or reliability
  • Technical investigation to resolve a qualifying uncertainty, even when it means diagnosing unexpected system behaviour
  • Projects that did not achieve the intended advance

Assessed project by project.

Not ordinarily qualifying

  • Features, interfaces and functionality built with established software techniques
  • Using existing AI models, libraries or APIs
  • Routine integration using established approaches
  • Routine debugging, maintenance and fixing ordinary defects

Commercial novelty alone is not an advance in technology.

Software, SaaS and AI R&D tax relief questions

Short answers first. Speak to us about your own position.

Unsure whether your build qualifies?Ask a specialist
Does developing a new SaaS platform qualify for R&D tax relief?

Not automatically. It may qualify where the project seeks an advance in technology and involves uncertainties that competent professionals could not readily resolve.

Can AI and machine learning development qualify?

Yes, where the development seeks a qualifying advance in science or technology. Using existing AI models, libraries or APIs does not automatically qualify.

Can unsuccessful software development projects qualify?

Yes. A project may qualify even where the intended technological advance was not achieved, provided the work genuinely sought to resolve qualifying technological uncertainty.

What evidence should we retain for software R&D?

Useful evidence includes architecture documentation, technical specifications, development tickets, code history, performance tests, experimental results and technical decision records. It should help establish why the uncertainty existed and how the company attempted to resolve it. See contemporaneous reporting.

Can we claim cloud computing, software and data licence costs?

Potentially. Revenue expenditure on software, cloud computing services and data licences may qualify where it is attributable to activities that directly contribute to resolving the technological uncertainty. Read the qualifying costs guide.

Can outsourced software development or contractor costs qualify?

Potentially, but the answer depends on the contractual arrangements, who decided or intended that the R&D would be undertaken, and where the activity was carried out. Contracts, statements of work and the surrounding facts should be reviewed before costs are included or the right to claim is assumed. Read about the contracted-out R&D rules.
