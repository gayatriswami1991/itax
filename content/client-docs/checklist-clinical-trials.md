# Clinical Trials & R&D Tax Relief

PRE-TRIAL CHECKLIST

Use this checklist to identify R&D tax considerations early, clarify arrangements and plan the evidence you will need before finalising a clinical trial. It covers five areas: which clinical trial costs may qualify; how CRO and sponsor contracts change who claims; evidence to keep from protocol to results; overseas work and subcontractor points; and questions to settle before a trial starts.

| ✓ CHECKED  Understood and documented | ? REVIEW  Further analysis may be needed | ! ACTION  Address before finalising |
|---|---|---|

This is a readiness check, not an R&D eligibility score. One significant issue may warrant further review.

| 1 | Which clinical trial costs may qualify? |
|---|---|

| GUIDANCE  Not all clinical trials involve qualifying R&D. Assess whether the work seeks a scientific or technological advance through resolving uncertainty before identifying qualifying costs. Phases I–III commonly involve such work; Phase IV activities require careful assessment against the same criteria.  Not every cost associated with a clinical trial is qualifying for R&D expenditure. Each item should fall within a permitted cost category and relate to qualifying R&D activities. Relevant payments made to subjects in return for participating in a qualifying clinical trial is a specific qualifying category. |

| 2 | CRO & sponsor arrangements: who may claim? |
|---|---|

| GUIDANCE  For accounting periods beginning on or after 1 April 2024, contracted-out R&D rules apply. Who may claim is not determined simply by who performs or pays for the work. The parties, contract and surrounding circumstances must be considered. |
|---|

|---|---|
| CRO agreement adequately describes the work | ☐ |
| Sponsor and CRO responsibilities understood | ☐ |
| Contractor and EPW arrangements distinguished | ☐ |
| Contract and surrounding circumstances considered to determine whether the customer intended or contemplated R&D of this sort to meet its requirements | ☐ |
| R&D tax treatment considered before signing | ☐ |

| ? REVIEW | R&D responsibilities are unclear, or several parties commission or perform the research. |

| 4 | Overseas activities & subcontractors |
|---|---|

| GUIDANCE  Restrictions apply to overseas contractor and EPW expenditure. An exception may apply where conditions necessary for the R&D are not present in the UK and it would be wholly unreasonable to replicate them here. Cost or workforce availability alone is not sufficient. |
